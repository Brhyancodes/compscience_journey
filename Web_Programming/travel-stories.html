Destination Stories & Attraction Guides

Paris – The Eiffel Tower

Constructed for the 1889 World's Fair, the Eiffel Tower stands 330 meters tall on the Champ de Mars. Visitors can ascend three levels for panoramic views of the Seine River and Paris's historic boulevards.

Rome – The Colosseum

Completed in 80 AD, the Flavian Amphitheatre—known globally as the Colosseum—is the largest ancient amphitheater ever built. It remains an iconic symbol of Imperial Rome's architectural and engineering mastery.

New York – The Statue of Liberty

Gifted by France to the United States in 1886, Liberty Enlightening the World stands on Liberty Island in New York Harbor, welcoming travelers and symbolizing freedom and democracy.
